Rice Bag — 8 Side Seal

Airtight seal, protecting the rice from moisture, oxygen and other contaminants.

Overview

Rice Bag — 8 Side Seal 八边封米袋

The flat-bottom, eight-seal construction is the most premium rice pack we make. Side and bottom gussets give it four printable faces and a base flat enough to stand square on shelf.

V-cut opening is standard. At 0.14 gauge it carries a 5kg fill without help from an outer carton.
